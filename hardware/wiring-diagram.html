Double-crimping

◆ Two wires into one terminal

Daisy-chaining moves each branch to the pass-through pin, where an incoming and an outgoing wire meet. Join them by crimping both wires into one terminal — it works when the combined copper fits the barrel (rule of thumb: two conductors about one size class below the terminal's max, e.g. two 26 AWG in a 22–26 AWG terminal).

Strip both, twist together (tin lightly if solid-core), seat fully, crimp, and tug-test each leg separately. If a barrel won't take two: use a pigtail Y-splice (two → one, heat-shrunk) just upstream, or a small 3V3/GND distribution point. Bridging VCC→LED on the display and landing buzzer − on a spare GND keep the whole TFT connector single-wire.

⚠ PN5180 is 3.3 V only — never 5 V. PN5180 + TFT share MOSI/SCK/MISO; a firmware mutex arbitrates the bus.
